<p>Homework is any learning activity in your child's curriculum subjects
that is assigned to be completed outside of school hours. This includes
both written work as well as tasks done online or through digital platforms.</p>
<p><strong>Key Objectives</strong>
<br>Homework reinforces learning and deepens your child's understanding of
lessons taught in school, allowing them to regularly practise and apply
skills and concepts. It also helps to develop independent learning skills,
encouraging students to take ownership of their learning. In the process,
students develop important self-management competencies such as good study
habits, responsibility, and time management.</p>

<p><strong><br>Homework During Breaks</strong>
<br>During the March and September breaks, no homework will be assigned to
non-graduating students, giving them time to pursue self-directed learning
and co-curricular interests.</p>
<p>For the June and December breaks, homework will be capped at 50% of the
usual daily school week load. Graduating cohorts may be assigned up to
50% of the usual load during non-protected holiday periods.</p>
<p><strong>Role of Students</strong>
<br>To get the most out of homework, students are encouraged to understand
what is expected of them for each assignment and manage their time well
by recording deadlines in their student handbook. They should approach
homework with the intention to learn, applying skills and concepts taught
in class, and take time to review teacher feedback to understand how they
can improve.</p>
<p><strong>Role of Parents</strong>
<br>Parents play an important part in supporting their child's learning at
home. We encourage you to provide a conducive environment for studying
and to keep an eye on your child's overall well-being, helping them balance
schoolwork and other activities. Working closely with your child's teachers
and maintaining open communication will go a long way in supporting their
progress. Where possible, encourage your child to take ownership of their
homework, as this helps them build good study habits and independence over
time.</p>
